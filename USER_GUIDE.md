# DVUSIPTV User Guide

This guide lists the main screens, controls, and actions available in DVUSIPTV. Some actions are available from a short press, while content actions are usually opened with a long press.

## Basic Controls

### Android TV remote or D-pad

- Use the directional buttons to move focus.
- Press OK, Enter, or the center button to select the focused item.
- Press Back once to close a menu or return to the previous screen.
- Press Back from the player to leave playback and return to the previous screen.
- Hold OK or the center button for about one-half second on a channel, movie, series, episode, favorite, history item, or recording to open its action menu.
- On Live TV and Guide, press Up twice quickly while the channel list is focused to jump to the A-Z letter buttons. A single Up still moves normally through the channel list.

### Touch devices

- Tap a button, card, channel, movie, series, or episode to select it.
- Touch and hold a content card to open its action menu where supported.
- Use the visible Back button or the device Back gesture/button to return.

## Home

- Live TV: opens the live channel list.
- TV Guide: opens the program guide.
- Movies: opens movies.
- Series: opens TV series.
- Favorites: opens saved favorites and favorite groups.
- Search: searches Live TV, movies, and series.
- Account/profile button: switches between app profiles.
- Settings: opens app settings.
- Home favorite row: plays a saved favorite or opens its movie/series details.
- Continue Watching: resumes movies, series, or episodes where playback stopped.
- Recently Watched: opens recently played content.
- Recordings: plays recordings saved on the device or configured storage.
- Hold OK on Continue Watching or Recently Watched to remove an item from history.
- Hold OK on a recording to play or delete it.
- The bottom Refresh control performs the Home page refresh. It does not replace the full Settings refresh.

## Live TV

- All Channels: shows the complete live channel list.
- Favorites: shows favorites across favorite groups.
- Favorite groups: shows channels assigned to a specific group.
- Category buttons: filter channels by provider category.
- A-Z buttons: jump to a channel beginning with that letter when All Channels is selected.
- Double-press Up quickly from the channel list to focus the A-Z buttons.
- Select a channel to play it.
- Hold OK on a channel to open the Live TV action menu.

### Live TV action menu

- Play the channel.
- Record Now, when recording is supported.
- Record Weekly, when recording is supported.
- Add or remove the channel from Favorites.
- Add the channel to one or more Favorite Groups.
- Hide the channel.
- Add the channel to Multiview.

The channel card can also show current program information, catch-up availability, and favorite-group names when that data is available.

## TV Guide

- Select a channel row or program cell to focus it.
- Move left and right through the timeline to view other times.
- Move up and down through channels.
- The red current-time line stays at the current time while the schedule moves underneath it.
- Use the filter buttons to select All Channels, Favorites, or another available group/category.
- Use the Guide Refresh button to refresh guide data.
- Hold OK on a channel or program to open its action menu.
- Record Now records the selected current program when supported.
- Schedule records a future program.
- Record Weekly schedules a recurring recording.
- Add the channel to Favorites or a Favorite Group.
- Add the channel to Multiview.
- Hide the channel.

### Easier Guide playback options

Settings > Easy Start contains two optional Guide controls:

- Guide Auto-Preview Last Channel: turn this OFF so the last live channel does not automatically preview when the Guide opens.
- Guide Select: Play Fullscreen: turn this ON so one select opens the selected channel or program in the full player instead of previewing first.

Both settings preserve the existing behavior when left at their defaults.

## Movies

- Choose All Movies or a provider movie category.
- Sort by Newest, Title, Rating, or Watched.
- Select a movie to open Movie Details.
- Hold OK on a movie to open its action menu.
- Add or remove the movie from Favorites.
- Add the movie to a Favorite Group.
- Play the movie.

### Movie Details

- Play Movie or Resume Movie.
- Play From Beginning when resume data exists.
- Remove Resume to clear the saved position.
- Mark Watched.
- Add or remove the movie from Favorites.
- View available metadata such as rating, year, duration, cast, director, format, IMDb, TMDb, and trailer availability.

## Series

- Choose All Series or a provider series category.
- Sort by Newest, Title, Rating, or Watched.
- Select a series to open its seasons and episodes.
- Hold OK on a series to open its action menu.
- Add or remove the series from Favorites.
- Add the series to a Favorite Group.

### Series episodes

Hold OK on an episode to open these actions where available:

- Play.
- Play From Beginning.
- Play Next Unwatched.
- Clear Resume.
- Mark Watched or Mark Unwatched.

## Favorites

- View all saved favorites together.
- Filter favorites by All, Live, Movies, or Series.
- Select a favorite to play it or open its details.
- Create a Favorite Group.
- Rename a Favorite Group.
- Delete a Favorite Group when it is allowed.
- Move a Favorite Group up or down.
- Hold OK on a favorite item to move it to another group or remove it from the current group.

Favorites and watch history are profile-specific when using separate app profiles.

## Search

- Type a search term into the Search field.
- Press the keyboard Search/Enter key or the on-screen search icon to run the search.
- Press the microphone icon to use voice input inside the app.
- Use Recent Searches to repeat a prior search.
- Clear Recent Searches or Clear Search when needed.
- Filter results by All, Live TV, Movies, or Series.
- Select a result to play it or open its details.
- Hold OK on a result to open its action menu.

The physical microphone button on a remote is controlled by Android TV or Google Assistant on many devices. The in-app microphone button is the reliable way to search inside DVUSIPTV.

## Player

Tap the video or press OK to show the player controls.

- Play or Pause.
- Rewind 30 seconds for movies, series, and catch-up playback.
- Forward 30 seconds for movies, series, and catch-up playback.
- Previous channel or Next episode when available.
- Retry the stream.
- Audio: volume, mute, audio boost, and available audio tracks.
- Picture: Fit, Fill, or Zoom.
- Captions: select or disable available subtitle tracks.
- Back: leave the player and return to the previous screen.

### Live Menu

Live TV playback includes a Live Menu with:

- Add or remove the current channel from Favorites.
- Add the current channel to Favorite Groups.
- Channels: open the channel list without leaving the player.
- Quick Guide: view current and next program information.
- Previous Channel.
- Multiview.
- Catch-Up when available.
- Audio, Picture, and Captions.
- Stream Info: output, resolution, codecs, bitrate, buffer state, and player information.
- Retry Stream.

## Multiview

- Choose a 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6-tile layout.
- The focused tile provides audio.
- Select a tile to open its menu.
- Full Screen opens the focused tile in the normal player.
- Change Channel opens the channel picker for that tile.
- Use the channel picker filters and select a replacement channel.
- Save up to five Multiview Presets.
- Select a saved preset to load it.
- Delete Latest removes the most recent preset.
- Refresh reloads the Multiview channels.
- A saved-preset confirmation appears briefly after saving.

## Accounts and Profiles

- The Account button opens profiles.
- The main account can create separate profiles.
- Profiles can have separate favorites, Favorite Groups, watch history, and resume positions.
- Select Use to switch profiles.
- Select Remove to delete a profile when allowed.
- The normal provider login remains separate from app profiles.

## Settings

### Easy Start

- Simple Mode: large buttons for Watch TV, TV Guide, Favorites, Movies, Series, and More/Advanced.
- Startup Destination: Home, Live TV, Guide, Favorites, or Last Watched Channel.
- Resume Last Channel: remembers a successfully playing Live TV channel.
- Guide Auto-Preview Last Channel: controls whether the previous live channel previews when Guide opens.
- Guide Select: Preview First or Play Fullscreen.
- Auto Start: best-effort startup after device boot. Some Android TV devices block this behavior.

### DVR Recording

- Open the DVR screen.
- Choose a USB recording folder when supported.
- Browse Android storage when USB storage is unavailable.
- Recheck storage availability.
- View and remove scheduled recordings.

### Channel and Category Management

- Hide or show individual channels.
- Hide or show Live TV/Guide categories.
- Hide or show movie categories.
- Hide or show series categories.
- Reorder Live TV/Guide categories.
- Manage Favorite Groups.

### Home Favorites

Choose which Favorite Group appears as a Home screen row.

### Playback

- Live Output Format: Auto, TS, or HLS.
- Playback Server: choose an available provider server.
- EPG Days to Load: 1, 2, or 3 days.
- Player Overlay Timeout: 3, 5, 8, or 10 seconds.
- Auto-Resume Playback for movies and series.
- Auto-Play Next Episode.

### Network and Updates

- Run the Internet Speed Test. It uses a public test download and does not send provider credentials.
- Check for app updates.
- Download an available update.
- View the installed and latest version.

### Parental Controls

- Hide adult categories.
- Set a 4-8 digit parental PIN.
- Require the PIN to show adult content.
- Disable the parental PIN with the current PIN.

### Data Management

- Refresh All Content: reloads provider catalogs and may take a while.
- Create Backup.
- Restore a local backup.
- Save a backup to USB when supported.
- Import a backup from USB when supported.
- Delete local backups.
- Clear Favorites.
- Clear Watch History.
- Sign Out.

Backups include settings, favorites, hidden channels, and watch history. Provider credentials are not included in backups.

## Diagnostics and Private Provider Inspector

Diagnostics can show:

- App version, device, Android version, and free storage.
- API connection status.
- EPG cache age.
- Current stream title and type.
- Buffer state, codec, resolution, bitrate, and dropped frames.
- Playback failure history.
- Provider health checks for Live TV, Movies, and Series APIs.

## Refresh Buttons

- Home Refresh: refreshes Home-related content.
- Live TV Refresh: refreshes Live TV data and its guide summaries.
- Guide Refresh: refreshes guide data.
- Movies Refresh: refreshes movie data.
- Series Refresh: refreshes series data.
- Multiview Refresh: refreshes Multiview channel data.
- Settings > Refresh All Content: refreshes the complete provider catalog and can take substantially longer.
